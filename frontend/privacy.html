Privacy Policy Terms of Service

1. 100% Self-Hosted & Zero Data Monetization

DivinityAI is an open-source, fully self-hosted Islamic knowledge and Quran/Hadith retrieval platform. All conversation queries, saved bookmarks, salah prayer city settings, and user session histories reside strictly on your self-hosted server instance and your browser's local storage.

We do not track, collect, monetize, profile, or sell your personal information or spiritual inquiries to data brokers, marketing platforms, or third-party advertisers.

2. AI Question Answering via OpenRouter

To evaluate natural language queries, verify citations, and synthesize answers grounded strictly in scripture, DivinityAI connects to foundation language models through OpenRouter.

When you submit a question in the AI Q&A interface:

  • Only the text of your query and relevant verse/hadith candidate snippets retrieved from the local database are dispatched to OpenRouter for inference.
  • Your host system credentials, internal database files, and unrelated network parameters remain isolated and private.

3. Local Storage & User Autonomy

Prayer coordinates and city selections are stored directly in your browser's localStorage. Because DivinityAI is hosted privately, administrators and users retain absolute control to purge chats or reset database volumes at any time.
